# MnVision 360 — Data Dictionary

This document outlines the 25 core tables created in PostGIS for the MnVision 360 platform.

## 1. Registry & Governance
- `data_sources`: Master catalog of all datasets (satellite, geophysics, geology, simulation) along with provider, coverage, resolution, licensing, and availability status.
- `model_versions`: Registry of trained ML models, hyperparameters, validation scores (ROC-AUC, Precision@K), and MLflow tracking references.
- `users`: User authentication, roles (`admin`, `geologist`, `mining_engineer`, `viewer`), and access control.

## 2. Geological & Spatial Data
- `mines`: Spatial footprints, centroids, depths, annual capacities, and operational status for mining leases.
- `mine_blocks`: Sub-mine production units, level depths, estimated tonnages, Mn/Fe grades, and multi-factor operational readiness status.
- `geological_layers`: Spatial layers representing mapped faults, lineaments, lithological contacts, and geomorphic zones.
- `geological_units`: Categorical rock formations, lithologies, and geological ages.
- `mn_occurrences`: Authoritative known manganese occurrences (positive samples) and background reference points (unlabelled background).
- `boreholes`: Exploration drill hole collar coordinates, azimuths, dips, and total depths.
- `assays`: Interval chemistry (Mn%, Fe%, SiO2%, Al2O3%, P%) and lithological descriptions from borehole cores.
- `satellite_scenes`: Catalog of optical and radar satellite granules (Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, Landsat).
- `satellite_features`: Grid-cell spectral indices, textural metrics, and quality/cloud flags.
- `geophysical_surveys`: Spatial boundaries and metadata for airborne magnetic, gravity, or EM surveys.
- `geochemical_samples`: Point geochemical assays from rock chip, soil, or stream sediment programs.

## 3. Operations & Equipment (Simulation / Prototype)
- `weather`: Meteorological records (rainfall, temperature, humidity, soil moisture) around mine sites.
- `equipment`: Fleet records (excavators, dumpers, drills) with model, capacity, and current operational state.
- `equipment_events`: Timestamped telemetry events, maintenance interventions, and breakdown logs.
- `operational_delays`: Categorized shift disruptions (power, blasting clearance, weather, haulage congestion).
- `maintenance_history`: Service logs, component replacements, technician notes, and maintenance expenses.
- `production`: Shift and daily production tallies, target vs. actual ore tonnages, average grades, and cycle times.
- `block_readiness`: Historical snapshots of development, drilling, blasting, and access readiness scores for mine blocks.

## 4. AI Predictions & Decision Engine
- `prospectivity_predictions`: Fine-grid mineral prospectivity scores, uncertainty estimates, and SHAP explanations.
- `drill_targets`: Clustered polygon targets, priority tiers (VERY_HIGH, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW), and accessibility constraints.
- `shortfall_predictions`: Projected 7/15/30-day production deficits, shortfall probabilities, and model-attributed risk factors.
- `recommendations`: Actionable recommendations generated by the Decision Engine awaiting engineer approval.
